PROLOGUE

A Story Before the Beginning

Every story has a beginning.

But what if the beginning was not where we were taught to look?

What if, before the first crown was raised, before the first kingdom claimed the heavens, before the first voice declared itself the keeper of creation, there was a mystery that belonged to no throne?

This is the story of the Undivided Mother.

She is the beginning before beginnings, the possibility before form, the silence before a name is spoken. She is not offered here as a forgotten fact carved from history, but as a myth through which we may explore creation, memory, identity, and the freedom to become.

In these pages, a Daughter searches for the truth of her origins. A Serpent teaches her the wisdom of transformation. Ancient fragments whisper of names forgotten, voices overlooked, and stories that survive even when their endings are lost.

And above them all stretches the sky, vast and unfinished, reminding us that every map is a choice about what to draw, every name is smaller than the life it names, and every mystery deserves both wonder and honest questioning.

This is not a story asking you to abandon what you know.

It is an invitation to look again.

To question without fear.
To remember without being imprisoned.
To honor the past without surrendering the future.
To recognize that difference need not become domination, and that love need not become ownership.

Most of all, it is a story about the possibility of wholeness.

Not a wholeness in which every voice becomes the same, but one in which no voice must disappear for another to be heard.

So come to the waters.

Listen for the Serpent moving beneath the surface. Watch for the Daughter standing beneath thirteen doors of possibility. And when the Mother speaks, do not hurry to decide what she must mean.

Let the story unfold.

For before the first father spoke his name, the Mother knew the mystery of life.

And before the first page was turned, the beginning was already waiting.
